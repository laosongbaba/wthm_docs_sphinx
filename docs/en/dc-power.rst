Power Supply
============

The device supports two power supply options:

1. Standard USB Type-C 5V power supply. Rated operating current 100mA, peak operating current less than 200mA.
2. DC power terminal supply, rated input voltage DC12V, range DC9~28V.

Using DC Power Supply
---------------------

To use DC power, remove the 3 screws securing the bottom shell and the front cover to expose the main board. The DC power terminal is located on the power board next to the USB connector.

Connect the DC power according to the polarity marked on the silkscreen. Rated input voltage DC12V, range DC9~28V.
